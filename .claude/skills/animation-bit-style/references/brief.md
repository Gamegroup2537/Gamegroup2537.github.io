# The brief

Collect these before building. Items marked * are required; draft the rest yourself and confirm.

| # | Item | Why it is needed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | * **One question** the clip answers ("why does X...", "which Y gives Z") | The whole clip is the answer to one question. Two questions make two clips. |
| 2 | * **The answer in one sentence** | Shown at the end; everything before it builds to it. |
| 3 | * **3-4 supporting facts**, each one short line | One per section. More than four will not fit in 32 s. |
| 4 | * **The subject to show** and what is inside it | Decides the modelling work. Ask for reference pictures of the real thing. |
| 5 | **What is being compared** (2-3 things) | These get the accent colours and the rows of the chart. |
| 6 | **What number changes** during the clip | Feeds the gauge and the bars (an angle, a speed, a load, a percentage). |
| 7 | **Audience and platform** (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) | Sets the tone of the wording and how much of the bottom of the frame the app covers. |
| 8 | **Language**, and whether technical terms stay in English | Captions are short; mixed Thai + English terms is common and fine. |
| 9 | **Channel name / watermark** | `WATERMARK` in the timeline. Never copy another channel's mark. |
| 10 | **Call to action** | The last four seconds. |
| 11 | **Cover headline**: the result in 1-2 words, plus a promise with a number in it | The cover is what gets the click. Draft it from items 1-3 if the user has none. |
| 12 | **Source for the facts** | So claims can be checked. Flag anything that is contested or simplified. |

## Shape of the 32 seconds

Keep this structure unless the user asks otherwise; the music, the slow-motion and the reveal are timed to it.

| Time | Beat | Screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3 s | Hook | Subject in action at real speed, the question as a big caption |
| 3-4 s | Slow down | Time slows, the scan sweeps the outside away |
| 4-9 s | What is inside | Camera orbits, parts are named one at a time |
| 9-14 s | Fact 1 | Demonstration + one instruction line + label(s) + callout |
| 14-19 s | Fact 2 | same |
| 19-24 s | Fact 3 | same |
| 24-28 s | Speed up + summary | Checklist and summary chart |
| 28-32 s | Answer + call to action | Outside view again, ends on the first frame |

Captions are two lines of at most about 22 Thai characters each; the second line is the highlighted one. Labels are 2-4 words. Callouts are one word.

## Filled example (the template clip)

- Question: อยากไหล่กลม ทรงสวย ต้องฝึกท่าไหน?
- Answer: ฝึกเดลทอยด์ให้ครบ 3 มัด
- Facts: ไหล่ = เดลทอยด์ 3 มัด / มัดกลาง -> Lateral Raise / มัดหลัง -> Reverse Fly / มัดหน้า -> Overhead Press
- Subject: a person lifting dumbbells; inside: skeleton, muscles, the deltoid in three colours
- Compared: front / middle / rear head (cyan / pink / green)
- Number: arm angle, and how hard each head is working
- Call to action: เซฟไว้ แล้วไปลองที่ยิม
- Simplifications to disclose: activation percentages are illustrative, not measured; muscles are simplified shapes
